# 🛠️ Curated Core Stack

This document outlines the software, frameworks, and languages I have **adopted** for my professional and creative workflows. These tools are selected for their performance, scalability, and long-term viability.

## 💻 Development & Frameworks
- **Python & TypeScript**: Primary for AI logic, backend systems, and type-safe frontend development.
- **Next.js & Vue.js**: Chosen for their superior DX (Developer Experience), SEO optimization, and reactive state management.
- **Dart & Kotlin**: For cross-platform and native mobile application architecture.

## ⚙️ Infrastructure & Systems
- **Docker & Kubernetes**: Essential for immutable environment orchestration and horizontal scaling.
- **Linux (Ubuntu/Debian)**: The backbone of my server environments, chosen for stability and architectural control.
- **Git & GitHub Actions**: Core for version control and automated CI/CD pipelines.

## 🎨 Creative Production (Subscription-Free Suite)
- **DaVinci Resolve**: Professional non-linear video editing, color correction, and audio post-production.
- **Affinity Photo**: High-performance raster editing and professional photo retouching.
- **Darktable**: Advanced RAW photo processing and workflow management.
- **Blender**: 3D modeling, rendering, and complex motion graphics.

## 🤖 AI, Audio & Character Logic
- **ComfyUI**: Modular internal logic for Stable Diffusion and generative AI experiments.
- **LMMS & Audacity**: Open-source music production (DAW) and surgical audio editing.
- **VRoid & Warudo**: 3D character construction and anime-style scene orchestration.
