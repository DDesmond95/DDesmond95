# 🔍 Workflow Observations & Alternatives

This document logs software, patterns, and frameworks I have **evaluated** but chose alternative paths for. These decisions are made to favor efficiency, cost-benefit ratios, or architectural freedom.

## 💻 Technical Debt & Legacy Workflow
- **Legacy Java / Unmanaged C**: Often introduces significant boilerplate overhead compared to modern Python/TS for rapid dev cycles.
- **JQuery / Backbone**: Avoiding the fragmentation and imperative state management that often comes with legacy DOM manipulation.

## ⚙️ Architectural Observations
- **Manual VM Management**: Moves away from the reproducibility of immutable containers; creates "snowflake" server technical debt.
- **Proprietary OS Lock-in**: Avoiding systems that restrict deep architectural control and high-performance GPU stacking.

## 🎨 Creative Alternatives (Evaluated)
- **Adobe Creative Cloud Suite**: After extensive evaluation, I have transitioned away to avoid permanent subscription lock-in and heavy resource overhead.
- **Autodesk Maya / Cinema 4D**: Highly capable tools, but avoided in favor of Blender's superior open-source ecosystem and rapid community-driven development.

## 🤖 Proprietary AI & Audio Models
- **Gated Proprietary APIs**: Avoiding vendor lock-in where possible; favoring local control and open-weights models (via Hugging Face & local LLMs).
- **Subscription-based DAWs**: Preferring one-time purchase or open tools (LMMS/Audacity) to maintain total ownership of the creative logic and production pipeline.
- **Paid Character Tools**: Evaluations led away from subscription-taxed character systems toward open VRoid/Warudo ecosystems.
